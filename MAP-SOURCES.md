# Map sources

Country outlines in `MapData.js` come from [Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 countries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/), retrieved on 2026-09-08 from the [Natural Earth vector repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson). Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

The bundled subset includes Belgium, the European Netherlands, and neighboring France, Germany and Luxembourg. Coordinates are rounded to four decimal places; only exterior rings are retained. City markers are approximate city centers for navigation, not address lookup results. The map is a regional overview, not a precise coverage boundary or a live rain overlay.
